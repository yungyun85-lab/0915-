# FocusLingua — Stitch Prompts

## 色彩系統（套用於以下所有畫面）
Base palette: warm off-white background `#FAF8F4` (not pure white, reduces glare/eye strain). Primary accent: dusty sage green `#7A9B7E` (calming, not clinical, distinct from typical "medical teal"). Secondary accent: warm terracotta `#D97D54` (used sparingly for the single primary CTA button per screen, warm and inviting rather than alarming). Success/streak: warm amber `#E8A33D` (not red-adjacent, avoids anxiety association). Text: soft charcoal `#3A3A38` (never pure black). Neutral surfaces: `#FFFFFF` cards on `#FAF8F4` background. Avoid saturated blue and red as primary tones — reserve red entirely for nothing (no red anywhere in the app, including error states, to prevent triggering shame/failure association for learners with prior negative school experiences).
